What is XAU/USD?

XAU/USD is the price of one troy ounce of gold quoted in US dollars. "XAU" is the ISO code for gold, so when the XAUUSD price reads 4,070, one ounce costs US$4,070.

Gold CFD trading lets you speculate on that price in either direction without buying or storing physical bullion. Go long if you think gold will rise, or short if you expect it to fall, and use the XAUUSD live chart above to plan your entries and exits.
